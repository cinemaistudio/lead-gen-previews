About Us

Four Decades of Trusted Dental Care

National Medical Center has been Satwa's most trusted dental clinic since 1980.

Founded in 1980, National Medical Center has grown to become one of Satwa's most trusted dental care providers. With over 42 years of dedicated service, our team of highly competent dental professionals offers a comprehensive range of sophisticated dental solutions.

Our specialists in Periodontics, Prosthodontics, and Endodontics are committed to helping you achieve the healthy, beautiful smile you've always wanted. We believe that everyone deserves access to high-quality, affordable dental care.
