About Us

Who We Are

Your trusted dental care partner in Dubai since our inception

Exceptional Care, Lasting Trust

At Zoe Dental Clinic, we prioritize your dental health, offering personalized treatments and creating lasting relationships built on trust. Our clinic, also known as Alkaissi Medical Center, is located in the heart of Port Saeed, Dubai.

Our team of highly experienced dentists brings over two decades of expertise in prosthodontics, implantology, and general dental care. We are committed to making your dental experience comfortable, professional, and tailored to your unique needs.
