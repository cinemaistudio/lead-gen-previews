About Clairebelle

Your neighborhood beauty salon since 2017

Welcome to Clairebelle Ladies Salon, your premier beauty destination in Dubai Investment Park. Since 2017, we've been providing high-quality beauty treatments in a warm and welcoming environment.

Our experienced team uses only the finest products to ensure every treatment is unforgettable. From hair styling to skin care, we're dedicated to making you look and feel your best.

  • High-quality products guaranteed
  • Experienced and friendly staff
  • Convenient DIP location with parking
  • Personalized beauty consultations
  • Affordable pricing for every budget

Why Choose Clairebelle?

At Clairebelle, we believe beauty is natural. Our treatments enhance your natural beauty using premium products and proven techniques. Every visit is designed to be a relaxing, rejuvenating experience.

Located above Al Maya Supermarket in Ritaj, Dubai Investment Park 2, we're easy to find and conveniently accessible.
